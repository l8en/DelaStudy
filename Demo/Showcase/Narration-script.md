# DelaStudy — narration script

**Video length: 2:48.** Read only the quoted narration, not the timestamps.
The video is silent so you can use your own voice. Aim for a relaxed pace,
roughly 125–135 words per minute, with a small pause between sections.

### 0:00–0:10 · Introduction

> Hi, I'm M'hamed. This is DelaStudy, a study app I built to make learning feel more manageable.

### 0:10–0:25 · Choose a course

> The Library brings together high school subjects, selected AP preparation, and introductory college topics. I can browse the different levels or search for a subject. Here, I'll choose Geometry.

### 0:25–0:35 · See the path

> Each course has five units with a clear sequence of short lessons and practice. I can see my progress and start directly from the course page.

### 0:35–0:53 · Learn one idea

> Instead of opening a long article, a lesson introduces one idea at a time. Short explanations, highlighted terms, and useful examples make it easier to follow. I can move backward, and my place is saved.

### 0:53–1:18 · Put it into practice

> Then I put the idea to work with multiple-choice questions, fill-in-the-blank answers, and matching. If I get something wrong, the app explains it and lets me try again. Correct answers get clear feedback, and completing the activity saves my progress without awarding repeat XP.

### 1:18–1:36 · Explore resources

> When I want more depth, Resources stays separate from the lesson. Its dropdowns organize videos, textbooks, and full readings. I can open an offline chapter or watch a linked YouTube video inside the app without losing the course context.

### 1:36–1:48 · Check the sources

> The civics lessons reference Delaware Public Archives. Selected modules also show inspectable standards mappings, with their coverage limits clearly stated. This is practice, not state approval.

### 1:48–1:58 · Make a plan

> The planner turns my next step into a specific task. Here, I'm adding another review of angle relationships instead of leaving it as a vague intention.

### 1:58–2:18 · Focus and Zen

> From that task, I can start a timed Focus session and choose a sound, like brown noise, if it helps me study. Zen gives the timer more space, and I can hide upcoming tasks when I want fewer distractions.

### 2:18–2:30 · See real progress

> My profile brings together study XP, the level ring, and academic achievements. These reflect recorded activity, so the progress I see comes from work I've actually done.

### 2:30–2:40 · Prepare for college

> The college section helps organize my interests, application-planning steps, and questions for a counselor, with links to trusted resources when I need them.

### 2:40–2:48 · Close

> DelaStudy brings learning, practice, resources, and focus into one place. It's built around taking the next useful step.
