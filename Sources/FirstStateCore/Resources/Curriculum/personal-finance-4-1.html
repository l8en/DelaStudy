Debit, credit, and account fees

A debit-card purchase generally draws from a deposit account; a credit-card purchase generally borrows under a credit agreement. Similar-looking cards can therefore affect a budget differently. Review account fees, balance requirements, overdraft arrangements, interest terms, and transaction records. The product name alone does not establish its total cost. Financial protections and terms vary by product and situation, so consult current official guidance for a real dispute. Neither a credit limit nor an overdraft feature should be confused with free money.

Worked example

Suppose a hypothetical account charges $5 monthly while another charges no monthly fee but has other conditions. The first fee alone totals $60 per year; comparing only that one line would still omit any other relevant charges.
