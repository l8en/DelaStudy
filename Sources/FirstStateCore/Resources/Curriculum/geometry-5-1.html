Area by decomposition

A composite region can be partitioned into familiar nonoverlapping shapes or found by subtracting a missing region. A triangle's area is one half base times perpendicular height; a parallelogram's area is base times perpendicular height. The slanted side is not necessarily the height. State units, and check that the result is smaller than an enclosing rectangle when appropriate.

Worked example

A 10 by 8 rectangle has a right-triangle corner removed with perpendicular legs 4 and 3. The remaining area is 80 − (1/2)(4)(3) = 74 square units.
