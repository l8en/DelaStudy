Right-triangle trigonometry

For an acute angle θ in a right triangle, sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse, and tan θ = opposite/adjacent. Opposite and adjacent depend on which acute angle is selected. The hypotenuse does not change. Ratios are determined by the angle because right triangles with the same acute angle are similar. Use degree mode for degree measures.

Worked example

In a 3–4–5 triangle, choose the angle opposite the side of length 3. Its sine is 3/5, cosine is 4/5, and tangent is 3/4.
