Arithmetic and integer division

Java evaluates multiplication, division, and remainder before addition and subtraction, unless parentheses change the grouping. Division of two int values produces an integer quotient truncated toward zero. A double operand makes the numeric division floating point. Casting after an integer division does not restore its discarded fractional part. The remainder operator gives the remainder for integer arithmetic.

Worked example

7 / 2 is 3, while 7 / 2.0 is 3.5. (double)(7 / 2) is 3.0 because integer division happened first. (double)7 / 2 is 3.5.
