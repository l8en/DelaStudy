Read a financial-aid offer

A college's headline tuition is not the same as a student's complete cost or net cost. Compare relevant attendance expenses, including housing, food, books, transportation, and personal costs, using consistent periods. Grants and scholarships differ from loans: borrowing can cover a bill now while creating a repayment obligation. Work-study is not simply an automatic discount already deposited into an account. Aid conditions and renewal rules matter. Use official current resources and the actual offer rather than assuming an example represents any particular school.

Worked example

In a hypothetical annual offer, total attendance cost is $20,000, gift aid is $8,000, and offered loans are $5,000. Cost after gift aid is $12,000. The loan is a financing option, not another $5,000 reduction in that cost.
