Circle length and area

A circle with radius r has circumference 2πr and area πr². A sector occupying angle θ degrees is θ/360 of the full circle, so its arc length and area use that fraction. Radius and diameter are different: d = 2r. Length uses linear units and area uses square units. Leave π exact until the final approximation. A complete circle is 360° or 2π radians; identify the angle unit before choosing a formula.

Worked example

For radius 6 and central angle 60°, arc length = (60/360)(12π) = 2π. Sector area = (60/360)(36π) = 6π square units.
