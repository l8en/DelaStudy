Simple and compound interest

Simple interest is calculated on the initial principal under the stated model. Compound interest also includes previously credited interest. To compare examples, identify principal, rate, time, and compounding frequency. A hypothetical rate is not a current bank offer or a guaranteed investment return. Fees, withdrawals, taxes, and changing rates can alter real outcomes. For annual compounding with an unchanged rate r, the model is A = P(1 + r)^t, where r is a decimal and t is years.

Worked example

For hypothetical principal $100 and 5% annually, simple interest for two years gives $110. Annual compounding gives $100×1.05² = $110.25. The extra $0.25 comes from interest on the first year's interest.
