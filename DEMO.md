# Three-minute demonstration

This path uses only bundled content and works offline.

1. **Problem and purpose (25 seconds):** Open on onboarding. Explain that official Delaware primary-source lessons are often delivered as teacher-oriented pages and PDFs; First State Lens turns one verified 1787 record into guided student practice. Point out the non-endorsement and on-device privacy statements.
2. **Real practice (75 seconds):** Choose a study intention, begin, and start **Read the record**. Answer “October 24, 1787,” submit, and show the sourced explanation. Complete the second question. Repeated taps cannot record the same question twice.
3. **Signature mechanism (50 seconds):** On completion, open **Trail**. Show that Read the record is demonstrated at 75%, Purpose & perspective is newly unlocked, and later lenses remain locked. Switch to Accessible list. Explain the visible prerequisite and scoring rules.
4. **Persistence and integrity (30 seconds):** Close and relaunch the app. The next recommendation and completed-session count remain. Open Progress to show that every metric comes from stored attempts.

If a clean demo is needed, use **Progress → Reset local progress** and confirm the destructive action.
